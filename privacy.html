Privacy Statement

This app works completely offline. Your photos never leave your device: editing and print-sheet generation happen locally in the app, and no photo data, file names, or usage data are collected, stored, transmitted, or shared with anyone. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers, and it has no in-app purchases. Questions can be sent through the contact information on the app's Microsoft Store listing page.
